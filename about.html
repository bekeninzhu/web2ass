About Panfilov Park and Zenkov Cathedral

Panfilov Park is one of the oldest parks in Almaty, and it is the home of the famous Zenkov Cathedral. The cathedral is built almost completely from wood, without using a single nail, which makes it a very unusual building. It was built at the start of the 20th century and survived a strong earthquake soon after. Today it is one of the main symbols of the city.

Key dates

  1. 1904 - construction of the cathedral started
  2. 1907 - construction finished
  3. 1911 - a strong earthquake hit Almaty
  4. 1930s - the building was used as a museum
  5. 1995 - the cathedral was returned to the church

Quick facts

  • Located in Panfilov Park, in the center of Almaty
  • One of the tallest wooden buildings in the world
  • Built without a single metal nail
  • Surrounded by old trees and walking paths
Panfilov Park is calm in the morning and full of people in the evening, but the cathedral always stays the center of attention.

- from a local city guide

Many locals call it the wooden cathedral, and visitors are always surprised by its bright colors.
